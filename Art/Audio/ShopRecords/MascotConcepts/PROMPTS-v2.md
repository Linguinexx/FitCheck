# Refinamento dos mascotes

## laje

Create ONE original monochrome mascot LOGO SYMBOL, centered on a perfectly plain WHITE square background, in solid pure BLACK ink only. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMERALS, no captions, no badges, no mockups. Image 1 is the user's DripLab mascot and is ONLY a graphic-style reference: expressive face, bold confidently designed silhouette, thick controlled black masses and white negative-space cuts, angular energy mixed with polished smooth curves, memorable streetwear/music identity. Do NOT copy that human character, hairstyle, headphones, horns or facial marks. Image 2 is our very rough concept sketch, only a subject/concept starting point: substantially improve its design and facial personality rather than tracing it slavishly. Deliver professional original mascot logo artwork, flat vector-like solid shapes with crisp antialiased edges, not a realistic render, not painterly, no shading, no gradients, no distress or grain, no tiny floating details. One compact unified head silhouette, substantial internal white gaps, generous white margin of at least 12% on every side. Eyes must feel deliberately designed and expressive; no generic clip-art/emoji. Only a head, no body, no scenery, no frame or extra props. Balanced but not rigidly mirrored.
Subject: a mischievous RACCOON head for a classic hip-hop record. A clearly readable oversized backwards baseball cap tilted on the head, recognizable raccoon eye mask integrated into the face design, confident half-lidded eyes, one cocked eyebrow, a sly asymmetric smirk with one small fang. Rich angular cheek fur and rounded ears, a crisp sculptural cap brim, cheeky self-assured streetwear personality. Keep the raccoon unmistakable, avoid a fox or cat. The cap should be a strong part of the silhouette, not a tiny tab on a bald forehead.
