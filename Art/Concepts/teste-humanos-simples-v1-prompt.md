# Teste de linguagem humana simplificada

Ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, geração nova sem usar as caricaturas rejeitadas como referência de imagem.

Referências consultadas: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3834090/YAPYAP/ e https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK/. O teste é uma interpretação, sem incorporação de assets desses jogos.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Fresh visual reset for DripLab human characters. Make ONE simple character art-direction test image: TWO original HUMAN avatars side by side, full body, FRONT THREE-QUARTER VIEW, one compact and one tall/thin. The user explicitly wants the low-detail goofy co-op videogame character feel of PEAK and YAPYAP ('friendslop'), interpreted as original HUMAN characters who wear contemporary streetwear. They rejected detailed angular human caricatures with sharp noses, sculpted jaws, conventional handsome cartoon faces and random triangular skin texture. Do not make another expressive Pixar/Dreamworks/Disney human character sheet. The design must look like a deliberately simple, awkward and endearing real-time 3D game puppet at gameplay distance. Let large googly eye whites and funny tiny pupils dominate a very sparse face. Human skin colors, normal simple human hair and human body: NOT aliens or animal creatures. Do NOT copy actual scouts, wizard robes, wizard hat, black void faces or any recognizable existing game character. Original humans borrowing the level of simplicity, comedic awkwardness and tactile primitive shape language only.
KEY SHAPES: head is a chunky roughly rounded rectangular block with soft corners, very simple plain smooth broad surfaces, not a perfect sphere or tall potato. Cheeks barely indicated, NO sculpted cheekbones or defined jaw muscles. Two big separate bulging off-white eyeballs are mounted on the front, slightly unequal in size, with tiny black round pupils looking a little to one side; very slight natural eye misalignment gives confused deadpan humor. Eye whites clearly larger than in ordinary human caricatures. Tiny triangular stub nose, tiny flat line mouth, very thin sparse eyebrows or none, tiny low-detail round ears. Hair is ONE or TWO solid dark simple shapes, no strands, spikes or polygon hair noise. Neck short, shoulders a little rounded/slumped, elbows gently bent, arms slightly too long, hands rounded mitten palms with a thumb and no individual finger anatomy. Feet broad simple rounded low-poly shoes. A whole human figure can be understood in a few simple connected volumes. The silhouette still supports a normal tee, separate trousers and sneakers. Human, but radically simplified rather than realistic anatomy or a fashionable cartoon portrait.
LEFT: compact adult human, medium-brown skin, head wider than tall with a subtly lopsided top, short dark side-swept hair as one block, short thick neck, wide simple tee, relatively short legs, palms hanging by hips, blank slightly uncertain facial expression.
RIGHT: tall thin adult human, natural light warm-brown skin, head slightly taller and narrower but same family of head form, very short dark flat haircut, shoulders drooping, long gangly forearms, longer separated legs, faint small sideways smile and a confused gaze. Make clearly different proportions while sharing the same graphical construction.
BOTH wear the exact same plain soft lavender oversized tee, dark desaturated blue straight loose trousers, chunky cream sneakers. NO ornate folds, seams, jewelry, hats, logos, accessories or streetwear graphics; one or two broad shading bands only. Flat matte opaque colors, extremely sparse painted pixel details only, smooth broad surfaces, no all-over triangle patchwork or low-poly polygon noise. Simple visible edge geometry at ears/hair/shoes, not dense facets over the whole face. No plush/felt surface, no fabric microtexture, no realistic skin gradients, no gloss, no cinematic light or ambient-occlusion-heavy render. Neutral flat realtime-engine lighting, pale gray background, restrained contact shadow. Wide composition, equally important figures, feet and heads fully visible, uncluttered layout. Small black heading 'TESTE DE LINGUAGEM' only, no other captions. Aim for quirky indie CO-OP GAME HUMANS, charmingly stupid blank looks, not cute baby dolls, not a polished fashion animation poster, not Miis with black oval eyes, not Roblox block-bodied figures. Output is a concept illustration, not an actual Unity screenshot.
```
